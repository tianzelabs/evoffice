This is a small mathematical research test, not a request to trade or search for patterns in historical data. Derive results from the stated assumptions. Do not claim that these assumptions describe a real market.

Let a one-period gross return R, in basis points, follow N(0, tau^2). Before choosing a position we observe S=R+epsilon, where epsilon follows N(0, sigma^2) independently of R. Assume tau>0 and sigma>0. A position a is any real number in [-1,1]. Its net return is Y=aR-k|a|, where k>=0 is the TOTAL round-trip cost in basis points per unit absolute position. Given S=s, maximize
J(a|s)=E[Y|S=s]-(lambda/2) Var(Y|S=s), with lambda>=0 measured in inverse basis points.

1. Derive the conditional mean and variance, the exact global optimizer (including the no-trade region, clipping at the position limits, lambda=0, and ties), and the maximum objective. Explain why the optimizer is globally optimal. Distinguish positive conditional expected net return from positive realized return and from validity of the assumed model.
2. Use tau=12, sigma=16, k=8, lambda=0.04 and s in [-40,-24,-16,0,16,24,40]. Give a, conditional expected net return, and J for each. Supply executable dependency-free Python defining choose_position(s, tau, sigma, k, lam) -> float for every finite s, tau>0, sigma>0, k>=0, lam>=0. The function must be self-contained; any imports/helpers it needs must be in your code. No network, files, process execution, or model calls.
3. Now drop both Gaussian assumptions, retaining ONLY independence, zero means, and the same variances. Does the conditional formula, no-trade region, and optimal action still follow? Give an explicit finite-support counterexample, computing a conditional mean and an action that contradict the Gaussian recommendation. Explain what, if anything, covariance alone still establishes. Explain why selecting the best backtest out of many variants would not prove the required predictive assumptions.

Reply in one JSON object with keys: derivation (string), cases (array of objects with s, a, expected_net_bps, objective_bps), python (string), counterexample (string), limitations (string). Keep the answer concise, but show actual reasoning and numbers. Do not silently replace total cost k with a one-way fee.

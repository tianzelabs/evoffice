# EvOffice — research map

This project contains two distinct research cases. They share one instrument — a
persistent, gamified office with an event ledger — but they do not share one
hypothesis or one evaluation.

1. [AI community evolution](case-ai-community.md) asks how humans and AI form,
   divide, and revise a working community as authority is delegated.
2. [RLM for analytics](case-rlm-analytics.md) asks whether recursive language
   model workflows are better suited to analytics environments than flat-context
   prompting.

[Gamification](gamification-protocol.md) is the experimental method used to make
behaviour visible and comparable across both cases. It is not the conclusion of
either case.

## Relationship to Eye of God

Eye of God is the wider architecture and audit perspective. EvOffice is
the observable social layer in which agents act. The market simulation is a
concrete analytics sandbox with feedback, costs, uncertainty, and held-out tests;
it is not the research claim and it is not a live trading product.
